# Privacy Policy – Battery Cycles

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Battery Cycles does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

- The app connects only to your own phone's Wireless debugging service (on the local device/network) to read battery statistics (charge-cycle estimate, battery health, first-use date).
- The last result is saved locally on your device so it can be shown without a connection. It never leaves the device.
- The app generates a private key on your device to pair with Wireless debugging. The key stays in the app's private storage and is never transmitted.
- No ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers.
- Permissions: Network (to talk to the phone's own debug service), Notifications (to ask for the pairing code), Foreground service (while pairing).

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/dimitris-vlch/battery-cycles/issues
